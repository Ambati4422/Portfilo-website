About me
A data analyst who treats accuracy as the product.


I'm Jagan Ambati, a Junior Data Analyst at ilogitek Business Solutions in Bengaluru, based in Hyderabad. I trained as an electronics and communication engineer (B.Tech, ACE Engineering College), and the habit of debugging a system until it behaves carried straight into my data work.


My work starts upstream of the dashboard: finding out why numbers disagree, fixing it at the source, then building Power BI reports that business teams can use without asking for a data pull. That approach produced a validation pipeline that cut reporting errors by 20% and automation that turned a 6-hour weekly refresh into under 1 hour.


Across Airbnb, HR attrition, retail performance and HRMS vendor pricing projects I have covered the full path from raw data to decision-ready insight with Power BI, DAX, Power Query, SQL, Excel and Python.
